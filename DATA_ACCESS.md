# Data Availability and Controlled Access

Individual-level clinical data, CT images, and segmentation masks are not publicly available because the ethics approval and institutional data-governance conditions do not authorize unrestricted public disclosure, and complete de-identification of longitudinal imaging-linked records cannot be guaranteed.

Deidentified patient-level derived features and model risk scores may be considered through controlled access. Requests should be submitted to the corresponding author and must include:

1. the research question and analysis plan;
2. the requested variables and study population;
3. evidence of local ethics approval or exemption;
4. investigator and institutional contact details; and
5. a commitment not to attempt re-identification or onward redistribution.

Requests will be reviewed by the data-access committee designated by the participating institutions. Access is conditional on scientific and ethical approval, execution of a data-use agreement, and use within an approved secure environment. Approved data will be limited to the minimum necessary fields. Raw CT images, segmentation masks, direct identifiers, and linkage keys will not be transferred.

For the anonymized manuscript, replace the access contact with `[DATA_ACCESS_CONTACT]`. After acceptance, provide the corresponding author’s institutional email and, if available, a request form URL.
